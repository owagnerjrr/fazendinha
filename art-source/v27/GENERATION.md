## Prompt dos gatos
Create an ORIGINAL precise game sprite atlas matching this warm detailed hand painted pixel art farm cat. Transparent background. EXACT regular grid of 4 columns and 5 rows, 20 full body cats total, each cat entirely inside its own equally sized cell with generous clear transparent padding. Columns are: facing DOWN toward viewer, facing LEFT, facing RIGHT, facing UP away from viewer (back of head and body). Rows are coat variations: black charcoal, ivory white, orange ginger tabby, blue gray, calico white black ginger. Consistent same feline anatomy, size, pixel density, amber green eyes, recognizable little ears paws whiskers and curved upright tail. Four paws standing, neutral pose, no accessories, no ground shadows, no text, no grid lines. Each cat occupies only 60 percent of cell width and 70 percent of cell height. Exact evenly spaced centers. This is functional in-game art, avoid vector flat shapes. Match the reference's original artisanal pixel clusters and shaded outlines.

## Prompt do cachorro
Create an ORIGINAL pixel art game sprite atlas matching the supplied friendly golden brown farm dog, same floppy ears, warm golden fur, lighter muzzle and chest, dark nose, green neck bandana, curled furry tail. Warm detailed artisanal 16-bit pixel clusters and shaded dark brown outlines, legible paws, ears and tail, consistent anatomy. Transparent background. EXACT even grid FOUR columns and THREE rows, twelve complete dogs, equal cell spacing and size, generous transparent padding. Columns: facing DOWN toward viewer, facing LEFT, facing RIGHT, facing UP away (back). Row 1: standing naturally on four paws. Row 2: affectionate seated pose, eyes softly closed, tail curled and relaxed. Row 3: playful bow with forepaws lowered and rump raised, ears perked, mouth slightly open. All directions depict the SAME dog. Each dog entirely in its cell occupying 60 percent width and 65 percent height. No text, labels, grid lines, props, scenery or cast shadows. Keep same native pixel density and natural body proportions throughout.

## Integração
As posições reais esquerda/direita do resultado dos gatos foram identificadas visualmente e mapeadas corretamente. Células recortadas individualmente, margem transparente removida, reduzidas nearest-neighbor para até 42×44 dentro de quadros RGBA 48×48, pés ancorados na linha 47 e alfa binário. PNGs finais: public/assets/v27/cat-{black,white,ginger,gray,calico}-{down,left,right,up}.png e dog-{stand,happy,bow}-{down,left,right,up}.png. Atlas indexados equivalentes em src/cat-directions.json e src/dog-directions.json permitem geração síncrona e cache das animações, sem aguardar imagem durante interação. Os oito quadros da caminhada deslocam somente as patas inferiores; a fase acompanha a distância real percorrida. Brinquedos, mão, cauda e efeitos são desenho Canvas nativo em pixel art.
